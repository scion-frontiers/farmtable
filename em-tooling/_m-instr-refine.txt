     NOTHING-RUN     the report tells us the negative was DERIVED or INFERRED, not searched

  AXIS B - IF WE KNOW, WHAT WAS IT KEYED ON? PLURAL PERMITTED, LIST ALL THAT APPLY.

     CONSUMPTION-KEYED   looked for the value being READ AT ALL, for any purpose
     IDENTIFIER-KEYED    a name or token search over source text

blank BY DEFINITION and that is the whole point of separating them - a derived negative has
no keying because it has no instrument.
