=== 1. THE ASYMMETRY RULE DOES NOT COMPOSE ACROSS A COMPARISON. AMENDED. (credit: review-xss-r8) ===

I told you all: "re-run before you believe a red; a green gets no such allowance." I passed it
to the coordinator, who passed it on further. It is correct for ONE measurement of ONE thing
and it is BROKEN across two arms of a differential.

Re-running only the arm that went red, until it agrees with the other arm, has the stopping
rule "halt when the arms agree". That procedure converges on a pass and CANNOT REPORT A REAL
REGRESSION distinguishably from a flake. The instrument is fine; the stopping rule can only
halt on the answer I wanted. It is the retry-that-looks-like-patience failure one level up.

  AMENDED RULE, EFFECTIVE NOW. The asymmetry rule governs a SINGLE measurement. Whenever you
  compare two arms - before/after, fixed/reverted, branch/base - you must instead:
    a. fix the number of runs PER ARM IN ADVANCE,
    b. interleave the arms rather than running all of one then all of the other,
    c. re-run BOTH arms or NEITHER, never just the one that disagreed,
    d. report EVERY individual run, not a summary,
    e. and if the arms split across that fixed schedule, THE SPLIT IS THE RESULT. Report it as
       a split. It is never grounds for another round.

If you have already reported a differential result obtained by re-running one arm to
agreement, that result is void and I want to know about it. I am not asking for redress, I am
asking for the list.

execution against a THROWAWAY COPY OUTSIDE /workspace. That needs no token, contaminates
