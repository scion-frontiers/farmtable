Key feature in this deploy:
- **Feature 40** (PR #112): Independent vertical scroll for the Inspector panel. The previous
  implementation had no scrollbar on the Inspector body — content below the fold (Comments,
  Change History) was unreachable. This fix applies `overflow-y: auto` to `sl-tab-panel::part(base)`
  and uses `min-height: 0` on `sl-tab-group` to allow proper flex shrinking, giving the Inspector
  its own independent scroll region.
