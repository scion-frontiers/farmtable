Key changes in this deploy:

1. **CLOSED-Task Solo Fix (PR #158)**: Selecting and soloing a CLOSED task in Dependency
   View previously showed "No dependency relationships" even when the task has real BLOCKS
   relationships (correctly visible in Tree View). Root cause: four independent places in
   `ft-dependency-view.ts` unconditionally filtered out CLOSED tasks, including the
   explicitly-selected one. The fix surgically exempts only the explicitly-selected CLOSED
   task (and its direct relationship targets) from the filter in Solo mode; unrelated CLOSED
   tasks and normal (non-Solo) behavior are unaffected.
