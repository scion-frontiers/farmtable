**1. The four-cell verification matrix, now five axes.** The leg reports 2 principals ×
5 axes, with a narrow-scoped principal built in-process, and states it is **proven
falsifying against the PRE-FIX tree**: all 5 wildcard controls pass on both sides,

That is the right shape for a proof — a matrix that passes everywhere on the pre-fix
tree proves nothing. Verify the claim itself: check out the base, run the matrix, and
confirm which cells actually fail there. **The wildcard controls are the part I would
attack first** — a positive control that passes for the wrong reason is a control that
controls nothing, and this project has shipped one before.

**2. The five mutation arms and the three-cause collapse.** The leg reports arm B
(unguard `terminal_label_stages.go:198` alone) and arm C (unguard `authorizationStage`
alone) each change nothing, and only arm E, addressing three contributors, reaches
parity. Its positive control: at `enabled=true` the harness discriminates 7 of 8 label

Reproduce or refute. **A refutation is the more valuable result.** Note the eighth
shape — the harness discriminates 7 of 8, and the leg does not appear to explain the
one it cannot. Find out what that shape is and whether its non-discrimination is
benign. An undiscriminated cell in an otherwise-discriminating harness is where a
false GREEN hides.

**3. The C2 `snapVocab` padding.** The leg took what it calls the stronger option
rather than the cheaper one: it padded `snapVocab` so that a literal reading of the
belt's claim now distinguishes, reporting **128 / 32768**, on the grounds that a
comment-only fix leaves the belt's claim unfalsifiable. Check both halves — that the
padding does what it says, and that 128/32768 is a discriminating ratio rather than an
arithmetic that happens to be non-zero.

**4. Everything the diff added as a pin.** Apply the count-neutral bar from the shared
block to each: hold the count fixed, corrupt identity, and report whether it goes RED.
A pin that only reacts to counts does not land. This bar was adopted after a measurement
showed 8 of 14 entries were replaceable with junk at a fixed count, GREEN.

**5. Delivery versus consumption.** Named recently and it applies to any table-driven
harness here. A harness can prove a loop was handed every entry and yielded every entry
and still be unable to observe whether the loop **body** did anything. The demonstration
was `if (label) continue;` as the first statement of a loop body — 27 fixtures
unexercised, suite GREEN, a *cheaper* mutation than the one the machinery was built to
survive. The matrices in this round are table-driven. Ask of each: **if the body were
gutted, would anything notice?**
