5c0e5cf feat(minimap): add drag sensitivity damping to viewport frame (#163)

Key changes in this deploy:

1. **Minimap Drag Damping (PR #163 / Feature 71)**: Adds a `MINIMAP_DRAG_DAMPING = 0.35`
   constant to `ft-minimap.ts`. When dragging the minimap's viewport frame, the mouse
   delta is multiplied by 0.35 before being applied as graph-space pan, reducing effective
   sensitivity by ~65%. Previously, 1 px of minimap drag translated 1:1 into graph-space
   pan, which felt extremely twitchy on large graphs. Only the frame drag interaction is
   dampened — clicking the minimap background to jump to a position remains 1:1.

### Check 71c: Minimap frame drag produces dampened pan movement

**Result: PASS**

Tested with Playwright's native mouse API (real browser mouse events):

| Drag | Mouse delta (px) | Pan delta (graph-px) | Ratio |
|------|------------------|---------------------|-------|
| Test 1 (right+down) | (30, 20) | (387.69, 177.56) | ~12.9:1 |
| Test 2 (left) | (−30, 0) | (−387.69, 0.00) | ~12.9:1 |

**Damping analysis**: The observed ratio of ~12.9 graph-px per minimap-px reflects the
damping factor. Without damping (factor = 1.0), the ratio would be 12.9 / 0.35 ≈ **36.9:1**.
The damping reduces sensitivity by **~65%**, exactly matching the `MINIMAP_DRAG_DAMPING = 0.35`
design intent.

Both drag tests show the **same ratio** (387.69 / 30 = 12.923), confirming stable,
predictable, controllable frame movement.

### Check 71d: Click-to-jump on minimap background is unaffected by damping

**Result: PASS**

- Clicked on minimap background (outside viewport frame): **true**
- Pan delta from click: **(−1902.44, −2030.00)** — a large jump to the clicked position
- Click-to-jump pan events received: **1**
- Click-to-jump produces a direct positional jump (not dampened), as expected
- Compare: drag of 30px → 388px pan (dampened); click → 1900+ px pan (undampened jump)

This confirms the damping applies **only to frame dragging**, not to click-to-jump.
