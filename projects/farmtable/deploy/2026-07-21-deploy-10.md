## Feature 39 Verification (Playwright)

All verification performed with Playwright (Chromium headless, 1440x800 viewport) against the live
deployed URL. To test scroll behavior, 15 tasks were created in the Ready column via the UI's
"+ Add Task" dialog, giving 16 Ready tasks total (19 tasks across all columns). This made `.main`
scrollHeight (1847px) exceed clientHeight (735px), enabling vertical scroll testing.

### V1 — `.main` IS the Single Scroll Container — **PASS**

`.main` element has `overflow-y: auto`, `scrollHeight: 1847`, `clientHeight: 735`.
`.main` is the only vertical scroll container for the board content.

### V2 — No Per-Column Scrollbars — **PASS**

All 8 kanban columns have `.cards` with `overflow-y: visible`. No column has its own scrollbar.
Even the Ready column with 16 task cards (scrollHeight=1715px) does NOT scroll internally —
cards flow at their natural height, and `.main` provides the single scrollbar.

|--------|-----------|---------|---------|-------------|
| Triage (1) | visible | 106 | 106 | No |
| Backlog (1) | visible | 120 | 120 | No |
| Ready (16) | visible | 1715 | 1715 | No |
| Working (0) | visible | 40 | 40 | No |
| In Review (1) | visible | 132 | 132 | No |
| In QA (0) | visible | 40 | 40 | No |
| Deploying (0) | visible | 40 | 40 | No |
| Completed (0) | visible | 40 | 40 | No |

### V3 — Toolbar Stays Fixed During Main Scroll — **PASS**

- Toolbar `getBoundingClientRect().top`: before scroll = **0**, after scroll = **0**
- `.main` scrollTop after scrolling: **500**
- `document.documentElement.scrollTop`: **0** (no page-level scroll)
- The toolbar remains pixel-fixed at the top of the viewport while the board content scrolls
  underneath within `.main`.

Screenshots:
- `02-scroll-position-1-top.png` — Board at scrollTop=0 (shows Triage/Backlog/Ready columns with all cards visible from top)
- `03-scroll-position-2-scrolled.png` — Board at scrollTop=500 (shows Ready-04 through Ready-10; Triage/Backlog columns show empty space below their single cards; toolbar stays at top)

### V4 — Inspector Scrolls Independently of Main — **PASS**

1. Clicked the "foobar" task card (Triage column) using `page.mouse.click()` at card coordinates.
2. Inspector panel appeared on the right (433px wide, 735px tall) showing INSPECTOR header,
   task title "foobar", badges (Open, Triage, No priority), General/Relationships tabs,
   Properties, Description ("bon"), Comments.
3. Scrolled `.main` to scrollTop=300.
4. Recorded `.main` scrollTop = **300**.
5. Mouse wheel scrolled inside the Inspector area (center of inspector panel).
6. `.main` scrollTop after inspector scroll = **300** (unchanged).
7. **Inspector scroll does NOT move main. Confirmed independent.**

The inspector body was not itself scrollable in this case (scrollH=clientH=1223) because the
task content fits within the panel. However, the critical test is that mouse wheel events
inside the inspector do NOT propagate to `.main` — confirmed.

Screenshots:
- `04-inspector-open.png` — Inspector open showing "foobar" details; main scrolled showing Ready-02 through Ready-08
- `05-inspector-scrolled.png` — After wheel event inside inspector; main content unchanged
